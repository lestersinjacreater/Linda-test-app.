|---|---|---|---|---|
| shared/test-vectors.json (63 messages) | Written by hand by the team + Claude Code, modelled on public M-Pesa, bank, KPLC and KRA message formats and on scam types described by the team | Project (hackathon) | 63 | Synthetic. Test only (normaliser, fingerprint, later scorer parity). Never used for training. Phone numbers are fake placeholders. |
| ml/data/processed/synthetic.csv (2,300 rows, regenerated by `make data`) | Generated by `ml/src/features/dataset/synthetic.py` from about 110 hand-written message templates with random amounts, names, codes and fake placeholder phone numbers (seed 42) | Project (hackathon) | all | Synthetic. `train` and `val` only (whole templates are held out for `val`). Never `test`: `test` is reserved for real, scrubbed messages. |
| Swahili SMS Detection Dataset ("Bongoscam", 1,508 messages: 1,000 scam, 508 trust) | Kaggle, henrydioniz/swahili-sms-detection-dataset. Tanzanian Swahili | Confirm on the Kaggle dataset page. Not redistributed: kept in git-ignored `ml/data/raw/` | 781 after cleaning and de-duplication (285 scam, 496 trust) | Real. `train`, `val` and `test` (near-duplicates grouped so a script never sits in two splits). Phone numbers, names, Tanzanian networks and currency masked by `ml/src/features/dataset/public.py`. Reported separately from Kenyan data. |
| SMS Phishing Dataset for Machine Learning and Pattern Recognition (Dataset_5971: ham, spam, smishing) | Mendeley Data, Mishra and Soni. International English | Confirm on the Mendeley Data page. Not redistributed | 5,292 (ham 4,805, smishing 487) | Real. `train`, `val` and `test`. The 489 "spam" rows (adverts, not fraud) are dropped. Same cleaning as above. Reported separately. |
| Kenyan scam SMS (`scam_detection_labeled.csv`, 736 messages, Kenya, English, Swahili and Sheng) | Salim Mwatsefu, IwazoLab: github.com/Salimmwatsefu/NLP-Scam-Detection (`data/raw/scam_detection_labeled.csv`, commit b38138c) | No licence file in the repository. **Used with the author's written permission (21 Oct 2026), wording verbatim in [`docs/permissions/salim-mwatsefu-kenya-sms.md`](docs/permissions/salim-mwatsefu-kenya-sms.md):** use, copy, analyse, modify and share derived datasets or results allowed; acknowledge the source; check licences before redistributing the original data or commercial use. Not redistributed: kept in git-ignored `ml/data/raw/kenya_sms_mwatsefu.csv` | 582 after removing 53 unsure and 101 duplicates (235 scam, 71 betting adverts, 276 other genuine) | Real. **We do not use the file's labels** (61% come straight from a keyword script). Claude re-labelled every message (`ml/data/labels/kenya_sms_labels.csv`: fingerprints and labels only, no text); the team spot-checks 40. Betting adverts are trained as not-a-scam and reported separately. 183 messages that look generated (short generic lures, one-line chats) are used for `train`/`val` only, never `test`. Phone numbers masked by `ml/src/features/dataset/kenya_sms.py`. `test` = 117 realistic messages, reported as its own source. |
| A team member's donated inbox (19,105 SMS, Oct 2024 to Oct 2026, Kenya; Safaricom, Airtel, banks, loan apps, betting, friends and family) | Donated by Lester Sinja (team member), who asked for it to be used to train Linda | Private: **never committed or shared**. Raw file, labels and processed rows stay git-ignored on the training machine (`ml/data/raw/team_inbox.csv`, `ml/data/labels/private/`, `ml/data/processed/team_collected.csv`); the donor keeps the labels file | 1,162 after dropping Safaricom staff messages (127), unsure (23) and too-sensitive messages (23: passwords, tax IDs, health, a minor's exam result), removing duplicates and keeping one message per group of near-identical texts (28 scams, 9 betting adverts, the rest genuine) | Real. Claude labelled every distinct non-verified template (964) by hand; messages from verified sender IDs (MPESA, Safaricom...) are genuine by their sender. Scrubbed before use by `ml/src/features/dataset/team_inbox.py` (names, family names, phone and account numbers, M-Pesa codes, e-mails). `train`/`val`/`test` 60/10/30 by group; reported as its own source `team_collected`. Only the trained model (hashed weights) and aggregate metrics leave the machine. |
| A second team member's donated inbox (11,965 SMS, Kenya; Safaricom, banks, loan apps, betting, friends and family) | Donated through Lester Sinja (team member) as an SMS donation export for training Linda; the donor is identified only by a hashed donor id | Private: **never committed or shared**. Same rules and git-ignored places as the first inbox (`ml/data/raw/team_inbox_2.csv`; labels appended to the private labels file) | 1,815 on its own after dropping Safaricom staff messages (29), unsure (68) and too-sensitive messages (10), duplicates and near-identical repeats (77 scams, the rest genuine or betting adverts); 2,726 for both inboxes together after removing texts both donors received and capping betting adverts at 15 per sender | Real. Claude labelled the new templates by hand (5,614: 4,923 genuine, 532 betting adverts, 91 scams, 58 unsure, 10 excluded). The donor's family names live in a private, git-ignored list (`ml/data/labels/private/donor_names.txt`) used by the scrubber, so no names are in the code. Same 60/10/30 group split; both inboxes are reported together as the source `team_collected` (model `2026.10.26-1`). |
