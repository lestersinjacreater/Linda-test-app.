What Linda does for youLinda inakufanyia nini

Linda warns. You decide. It never blocks or deletes anything.Linda inaonya. Wewe unaamua. Haizuii wala kufuta chochote kamwe.

Scam texts flagged before you open themJumbe za ulaghai zinatambuliwa kabla hujazifungua

Linda checks each SMS on your own phone and warns on the notification, with the reason. It even works with no internet.Linda inakagua kila SMS kwenye simu yako na kukuonya kwenye arifa, pamoja na sababu. Inafanya kazi hata bila intaneti.

Scam callers labelled before you answerWapigaji matapeli wanatambulishwa kabla hujajibu

A call from a number reported to Safaricom's fraud team shows a warning while the phone is still ringing.Simu kutoka kwa nambari iliyoripotiwa kwa kitengo cha Safaricom cha kupambana na ulaghai inaonyesha onyo wakati simu bado inalia.

Real Safaricom numbers get a verified badgeNambari halisi za Safaricom zinapata alama ya uthibitisho

So a caller pretending to be Customer Care cannot fool you. Only numbers on the official list get the badge.Ili mpigaji anayejifanya Huduma kwa Wateja asikudanganye. Nambari zilizo kwenye orodha rasmi pekee ndizo hupata alama hii.

Report a scammer in one tapRipoti tapeli kwa mguso mmoja

Your report goes to Safaricom's fraud team with the number and the type of scam, never the message itself.Ripoti yako inaenda kwa kitengo cha Safaricom cha kupambana na ulaghai ikiwa na nambari na aina ya ulaghai, kamwe si ujumbe wenyewe.

You protect other people tooUnawalinda watu wengine pia

When a few Linda phones catch the same scammer, Safaricom can warn everyone who got the scam, on any phone, even a basic one, within minutes.Simu chache za Linda zikimnasa tapeli yule yule, Safaricom inaweza kuwaonya wote waliopokea ulaghai huo, kwenye simu yoyote, hata ya kawaida, ndani ya dakika chache.

Install in 4 stepsSakinisha kwa hatua 4

  1. Tap the green Download button.Gusa kitufe cha kijani cha Pakua. If your phone says this type of file can be harmful, choose Download anyway. That is normal for apps that are not from the Play Store.Simu ikisema aina hii ya faili inaweza kudhuru, chagua Pakua hata hivyo. Hilo ni la kawaida kwa programu zisizotoka Play Store.
  2. Open the file linda-debug.apk.Fungua faili linda-debug.apk. Tap the download notification, or find it in your Downloads folder. Downloaded Linda before? Delete the old linda-debug files first, so you open the new one. Linda's Settings shows the build number to check.Gusa arifa ya upakuaji, au uitafute kwenye folda ya Downloads. Uliwahi kupakua Linda? Futa faili za zamani za linda-debug kwanza, ili ufungue mpya. Mipangilio ya Linda inaonyesha nambari ya toleo ili uhakikishe.
  3. Allow installing from this source.Ruhusu usakinishaji kutoka chanzo hiki. Android asks once: switch on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back and tap Install.Android huuliza mara moja: washa Ruhusu kutoka chanzo hiki kwa kivinjari chako, kisha rudi na ugonge Sakinisha.
  4. Open Linda and follow the welcome screen.Fungua Linda na ufuate skrini ya karibu. Allow the permissions, and use the battery steps shown for your phone brand so Linda keeps working with the screen off.Ruhusu ruhusa, na utumie hatua za betri zinazoonyeshwa kwa chapa ya simu yako ili Linda ifanye kazi skrini ikiwa imezimwa.

Is it safe to install?Je, ni salama kusakinisha?

Linda is a prototype made for a hackathon. It is not in the Play Store, which is why Android shows a warning. The app is built automatically from the public source code, and you can read all of it.Linda ni mfano uliotengenezwa kwa hackathon. Haipo kwenye Play Store, ndiyo maana Android inaonyesha onyo. Programu hujengwa kiotomatiki kutoka kwa msimbo wa chanzo wa umma, na unaweza kuusoma wote.

Something went wrong?Kuna tatizo?

Download stuck near the end: wait a minute, then look in your Downloads folder; on office Wi-Fi, try mobile data. "App not installed": an older copy from a different build may be on your phone. Uninstall Linda, then install again. No Install button: go back and allow installing from this source (step 3). Linda stops working when the screen is off: follow the battery steps for your phone brand in the app's welcome screen.Upakuaji umekwama karibu na mwisho: subiri dakika moja, kisha angalia folda ya Downloads; ukiwa kwenye Wi-Fi ya ofisi, jaribu data ya simu. "Programu haijasakinishwa": huenda nakala ya zamani kutoka muundo tofauti iko kwenye simu yako. Ondoa Linda, kisha usakinishe tena. Hakuna kitufe cha Sakinisha: rudi na uruhusu usakinishaji kutoka chanzo hiki (hatua ya 3). Linda inaacha kufanya kazi skrini ikizimwa: fuata hatua za betri za chapa ya simu yako kwenye skrini ya karibu ya programu.
