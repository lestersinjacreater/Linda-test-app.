LINDA

Stop scam messages before they fool you.Zuia jumbe za ulaghai kabla hazijakudanganya.

Linda checks each text on your own phone and warns you, with the reason, before you lose money. It works with no internet.Linda inakagua kila ujumbe kwenye simu yako na kukuonya, pamoja na sababu, kabla hujapoteza pesa. Inafanya kazi bila intaneti.

Install in 4 stepsSakinisha kwa hatua 4

  1. Tap the blue Download button.Gusa kitufe cha buluu cha Pakua. If your phone says this type of file can be harmful, choose Download anyway. That is normal for apps that are not from the Play Store.Simu ikisema aina hii ya faili inaweza kudhuru, chagua Pakua hata hivyo. Hilo ni la kawaida kwa programu zisizotoka Play Store.
  2. Open the file linda-debug.apk.Fungua faili linda-debug.apk. Tap the download notification, or find it in your Downloads folder.Gusa arifa ya upakuaji, au uitafute kwenye folda ya Downloads.
  3. Allow installing from this source.Ruhusu usakinishaji kutoka chanzo hiki. Android asks once: switch on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back and tap Install.Android huuliza mara moja: washa Ruhusu kutoka chanzo hiki kwa kivinjari chako, kisha rudi na ugonge Sakinisha.
  4. Open Linda and follow the welcome screen.Fungua Linda na ufuate skrini ya karibu. Allow the permissions, and use the battery steps shown for your phone brand so Linda keeps working with the screen off.Ruhusu ruhusa, na utumie hatua za betri zinazoonyeshwa kwa chapa ya simu yako ili Linda ifanye kazi skrini ikiwa imezimwa.

Is it safe to install?Je, ni salama kusakinisha?

Linda is a prototype made for a hackathon. It is not in the Play Store, which is why Android shows a warning. The app is built automatically from the public source code, and you can read all of it.Linda ni mfano uliotengenezwa kwa hackathon. Haipo kwenye Play Store, ndiyo maana Android inaonyesha onyo. Programu hujengwa kiotomatiki kutoka kwa msimbo wa chanzo wa umma, na unaweza kuusoma wote.

Something went wrong?Kuna tatizo?

"App not installed": an older copy from a different build may be on your phone. Uninstall Linda, then install again. No Install button: go back and allow installing from this source (step 3). Linda stops working when the screen is off: follow the battery steps for your phone brand in the app's welcome screen."Programu haijasakinishwa": huenda nakala ya zamani kutoka muundo tofauti iko kwenye simu yako. Ondoa Linda, kisha usakinishe tena. Hakuna kitufe cha Sakinisha: rudi na uruhusu usakinishaji kutoka chanzo hiki (hatua ya 3). Linda inaacha kufanya kazi skrini ikizimwa: fuata hatua za betri za chapa ya simu yako kwenye skrini ya karibu ya programu.
